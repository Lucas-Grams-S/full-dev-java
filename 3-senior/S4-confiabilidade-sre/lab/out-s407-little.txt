Little's law: L = lambda x W  (items in the system = arrival rate x time each spends inside)

rate (req/s) | time inside W | concurrent L (threads/connections busy)
          50 |         40 ms | 2.0
          50 |        200 ms | 10.0
          50 |       3000 ms | 150.0
         200 |         40 ms | 8.0

The ChaosLab case: 40 req/s with notifications at 3 s -> L = 120 threads wanted; the pool had 16.
With a 200 ms timeout: L = 40 x 0.213 = 8.5 threads: fits; at 100 req/s: 21 > 16: it does not (measured in S4.04).
