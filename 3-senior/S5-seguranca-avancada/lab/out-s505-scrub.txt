strict (prefix + length + Luhn): leaks = 2, false positives = 1 of 11 lines
Luhn only                      : false positives = 2 (the order id with prefix 70 is masked too)
Reading: a scrubber is a SAFETY NET, not the control. The control is not logging the PAN at all (and not storing it): the net still leaks when the digits are split or encoded,
and it masks innocent numbers that happen to pass the check (the price of failing safe).
A PAN you MAY show keeps at most the first 6 and last 4 digits (rule to confirm in the PCI DSS text itself): maskPan(4111111111111111) = 411111******1111
