## B) the lock queue: an ALTER waits behind a long reader and BLOCKS every new reader
--- ALTER with lock_timeout=0 (0 = wait forever)
  -- what pg_stat_activity shows while the three sessions wait (A = idle reader holding the lock, B = ALTER, C = innocent SELECT):
   pid | state  |   waiting_on    | blocked_by |                    query                     
  -----+--------+-----------------+------------+----------------------------------------------
   156 | active | Timeout:PgSleep | {}         | begin; select count(*) from users; select pg
   163 | active | Lock:relation   | {156}      | set lock_timeout='0'; alter table users add 
   170 | active | Lock:relation   | {163}      | select 'reader C got its answer' from users 
  (3 rows)
  
  [C SELECT] reader C got its answer
  [B ALTER] finished
  [C SELECT] waited 4610 ms (the long reader A holds its lock for ~6 s)
--- ALTER with lock_timeout=1s (0 = wait forever)
  [C SELECT] reader C got its answer
  [B ALTER] ERROR:  canceling statement due to lock timeout
  [C SELECT] waited 570 ms (the long reader A holds its lock for ~6 s)
